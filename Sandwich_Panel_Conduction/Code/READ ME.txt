Thermal Control and Heat Transfer Simulation 
Language: Python  
Author: Alejandro Fernández Ruiz, Space Systems Engineer

The purpose of this document is to explain the physical and mathematical fundamentals behind the Python code
developed to simulate heat transfer and thermal control in a sandwich panel, as typically found in the space industry.
An EBOX is installed on the panel, together with a heater designed to keep the electronic board within its survival
temperature range at all times.


================================================================================
CODE STRUCTURE
================================================================================

Simulations:

Section 1: analyses the temperature distribution across a panel using a
one-dimensional steady-state heat conduction model. It calculates the effective 
thermal properties of the panel, obtains analytical and numerical solutions for 
different spatial discretizations, compares both approaches to assess the accuracy 
of the numerical method, and plots the resulting temperature distributions.

Section 2: analyses the steady-state temperature distribution across half
and the full panel, considering localized heat generation in the EBOX and heater,
using analytical and numerical methods.

Section 3: calculates the steady-state temperature distribution of the 
panel while modelling the EBOX as an independent thermal node coupled to the panel 
through thermal contact conductance.

Section 4: simulates the transient thermal behaviour of the panel and 
EBOX using the explicit Euler method, accounting for heat conduction, thermal 
radiation and heater operation. It calculates the temperature evolution over time
and saves the final state for subsequent simulations.

Section 5: simulates the transient thermal behaviour of the sandwich panel
and EBOX using a thermostat with hysteresis to control the heater. It accounts for
heat conduction and thermal radiation, calculates the temperature evolution over time,
and saves the final thermal state for subsequent simulations.

Section 6: simulates the transient thermal behaviour of the sandwich panel
and EBOX using a Proportional-Integral (PI) controller to regulate the heater power
and maintain the EBOX temperature at a desired value. It accounts for heat conduction
and thermal radiation, calculates the temperature evolution over time, and saves the
final thermal state and controller histories.

To get the results, please, simulate section X, inside simulations folder.

config/
    ├── common.py
    │       └── Defines common thermal parameters, including heat
    │           dissipation values and boundary temperatures.
    │
    └── section_1.py
    │       └── Input parameters specific to Section 1.
    │
    └── section_2.py
    │       └── Input parameters specific to Section 2.
    │
    └── section_3.py
    │       └── Defines the numerical parameters for Section 3.
    │
    └── section_4.py
    │       └── Input parameters specific to Section 4.
    │
    └── section_5.py
    │       └── Input parameters specific to Section 5.
    │
    └── section_6.py
            └── Input parameters specific to Section 6.

models/
    ├── geometry.py
    │       └── Defines the sandwich panel geometry, including its
    │           dimensions and layer thicknesses.
    │
    ├── materials.py
    │       └── Defines the thermal properties of the panel materials,
    │           such as the thermal conductivity of the honeycomb core
    │           and CFRP skins.
    │
    └── thermal_properties.py
            └── Calculates the equivalent thermal conductivity of the
                sandwich panel and the volumetric heat generation
                resulting from the EBOX and heater dissipations.

physics/
    └── analytical_conduction.py
    │       └── Analytical solutions for steady-state heat conduction.
    │
    │
    └── conduction.py
             └── Analytical and numerical solutions for one-dimensional steady-state heat conduction.

numerical/
    └── discretization.py
    │        └── Finite difference discretization of steady-state
    │
    └── steady_state.py
              └── Numerical solvers for steady-state linear systems.

simulations/
    └── section_X.py
              └── Coordinates the Section X simulation. This files are compiled to get results of 
                   the different simulations.

postprocessing/
    └── plotting.py
            └── Contains the functions that plots results for all study cases.
